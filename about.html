About Stackly HR Partners

We exist to help ambitious
companies build deliberately

Stackly was founded on a simple, stubborn belief: that high-retention talent strategy, localized compliance, and human support belong under one roof. No outsourcing shortcuts. No compliance risk.

Meet our team
Our origin

Born from the frustration of hyper-volume recruitment agencies

In 2021, our founders were leading international talent divisions. They noticed a painful pattern: modern companies were forced to choose between massive agency retainers with zero tech capability, or SaaS software that isolated them with cold, automated candidate queues.

We built Stackly to bridge that chasm. By wrapping robust local compliance algorithms and curated sourcing networks around dedicated executive advisors, we gave growing teams a precise, reliable system to expand globally with absolute confidence.

Our core pillars

The principles behind our methodology

Deliberate action

We reject the hyper-speed 'spray and pray' resume volume strategy. Great teams are built with intent, slow filters, and structured alignment.

Global compliance

Geography shouldn't dictate legal risk. We build deep compliance directly into our sourcing algorithms so borders disappear cleanly.

Radical transparency

No hidden retainers, no mysterious margins. We provide clear, market-accurate salary index data to both hiring managers and candidates upfront.
